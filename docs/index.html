Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Also available in: Español · Português · Català

This Privacy Policy describes how My Days ("the app") handles information when you use it on Android or iOS. My Days is a daily calendar that shows sunrise and sunset times, moon phases, saints of the day, international observances, famous birthdays and historical events for any date up to today. Its daily content is researched with the help of Google's Gemini AI and prepared in advance.

In short:

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or GPS location.
  • It uses an approximate location, worked out from your IP address, to calculate sun and moon times for where you are.
  • Your settings are stored only on your device.
  • Our server keeps standard technical request logs for 30 days, and nothing else.

1. Information the App Processes

To show a day, the app requests that day's content from our server and sends:

  • The selected calendar date
  • The language you chose in the app

The app also uses an approximate location for your device (see Section 4). It does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise (GPS) location, advertising identifier or any account data.

2. Our Server and Where the Content Comes From

Our server runs on Google Cloud Run in the European Union (Belgium). For each request it returns content that was prepared in advance and published as public files on the jsDelivr content network. If a day's content is not available yet, the server asks the Google Gemini API to generate it. Only the date and the language are sent to Gemini, never anything about you or your device.

Like any web server, Google Cloud automatically records standard request logs: your IP address, the time of the request, the requested address (which includes the date and language) and your device or browser type. We use these logs only to keep the service running and to find errors. They are not combined with any other data, not used to identify you, and are deleted automatically after 30 days.

3. Third-Party Services

The app connects to the services below. Each of them receives your IP address and basic technical information as part of any internet request, and handles it under its own privacy policy. The app sends them nothing else beyond what is listed.

  • Google Cloud Run (hosting of our server) and the Google Gemini API (content generation, receives only a date and a language from our server). See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • jsDelivr (delivery of the public daily content files, also used by the daily notification, see Section 5). See the jsDelivr Privacy Policy.
  • ipwho.is and, as a fallback, ipinfo.io (approximate location from your IP address, see Section 4). See the ipinfo.io Privacy Policy.
  • Open-Meteo Geocoding API (receives the text you type when you search for a place, see Section 4). See the Open-Meteo Terms and Privacy.
  • Google Fonts and Transparent Textures (the app's fonts and paper textures, downloaded when the app opens).
  • Google Play on Android and the Apple App Store on iOS (checking whether a newer version of the app is available). See Google's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

The app does not include any analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking services.

4. Location

My Days uses an approximate location to calculate accurate sunrise and sunset times, moon phases and seasons for where you are. On first launch, and whenever you tap "Use my location" in Settings, the app asks ipwho.is (or ipinfo.io if it is unavailable) for an approximate location based on your IP address, usually at city level. The app never accesses your device's GPS or precise location services and never asks for location permission.

You can also search for a place by name in Settings. The text you type is sent to the Open-Meteo Geocoding API to find matching places.

The resulting location (coordinates, time zone and a label such as "City · Region · Country") is stored only on your device (see Section 7). It is not sent to our server.

5. Daily Notification

If you turn it on in Settings, My Days shows one local notification a day, at the time you choose, with a highlight about that day. The notification is created on your device. No push-notification server is involved.

To keep the notification's text up to date, the app downloads that day's public content file from jsDelivr, also in the background when the app is closed (through Android's background work scheduler, or iOS's Background App Refresh). These downloads contain no information about you other than the standard request information described in Section 3.

You can turn the notification off or change its time at any time in Settings, and revoke the notification permission in your device's settings.

6. Sharing the App

The "Share My Days with them" button opens your device's own share menu with a short message and a link to our download page. The app does not see whom you share with or which app you use. The download page is hosted on GitHub Pages (see the GitHub Privacy Statement) and only points visitors to the App Store or Google Play.

7. Data Stored on Your Device

The following is stored only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Your language choice and notification preferences (on or off, time, and the type of content you'd like to see)
  • Your location setting (Section 4)
  • A small record of the last scheduled notification, so the same notification isn't shown twice

This data is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

8. Permissions

  • Internet access, to load each day's content.
  • Notifications (asked only when you turn on the daily notification).
  • On Android: running at startup, so the daily notification is restored after your phone restarts, and an optional request to ignore battery optimization, so the notification arrives on time.
  • On iOS: Background App Refresh, which you can turn off in the iOS Settings app.

My Days does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Because My Days has no accounts and does not store personal data linked to you, there is no profile to view or delete. You can change or turn off the location, notification and language settings at any time, and uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device. Server request logs are deleted automatically after 30 days. If you live in the European Union or another region with data protection laws, you can contact us at any time to exercise your rights (see Section 12).

10. Children's Privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
