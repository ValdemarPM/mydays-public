Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Also available in: Español · Português · Català

This Privacy Policy describes how My Days ("the app") handles information when you use it. My Days is a calendar and historical-events app that uses Google's Gemini AI to generate content about selected dates.

1. Information We Process

When you tap on a date, the app sends the following to the Google Gemini API:

  • The selected calendar date
  • Your device language preference

To show sunrise/sunset times, moon phases, and seasons for your own location, the app also determines an approximate location for your device — see Section 4 below for details. No name, email, device identifiers, or account data is collected, stored, or transmitted by the app.

2. How the Data Is Used

The date and language are sent solely to generate AI-produced historical and astronomical information about that date. The response is displayed to you and is not stored or logged by the app.

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses the Google Gemini API to generate content. Data sent to this API is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. It also uses ipwho.is, ipinfo.io and the Open-Meteo Geocoding API for location detection and search — see Section 4 for details. The app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

4. Location

My Days uses an approximate location — derived from your device's IP address via the third-party service ipwho.is (or ipinfo.io as a fallback if it is unavailable) — to calculate accurate sunrise/sunset times, moon phases, and seasons for where you are, instead of a fixed default location. This lookup happens automatically once (on first launch, or whenever you tap "Use my location" in Settings) and is not linked to any other data the app processes. The resolved location (coordinates, timezone, and a human-readable label such as "City · Region · Country") is stored locally on your device only — see Section 7.

You can also search for a location by name in Settings to correct the detected location or to view another region's data. These searches are sent as plain text queries to the third-party Open-Meteo Geocoding API and are not linked to your IP address or any other data the app processes.

My Days never accesses your device's GPS or precise location services — only the IP-based approximate location described above, or a location you explicitly search for.

5. Data Storage

My Days does not maintain any servers that store user data. No account is required and no personal data is retained after you close the app.

6. Local Notifications

My Days can optionally send you a daily local notification with a highlight about the current date. This notification is scheduled entirely on your device using Android's notification system — no data is sent to us or to any third party to enable it, and no push-notification server is involved. You can enable, disable, or reschedule this notification at any time from the app's Settings, and you can revoke the notification permission from your device's system settings.

7. Preferences Stored on Your Device

Your notification preferences (whether notifications are enabled, the scheduled time, and the type of content you'd like to see) and your location setting (Section 4) are stored locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted to us, and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app.

8. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "last updated" date.
